# China TCG Secondary Market Mechanics (闲鱼-centric) and Cross-Game Popularity Indicators — as of 2026-11-05

> **Method and evidence limits (read first).** In this research session WebFetch was blocked for every host tried (finance.sina.com.cn, jihuanshe.com, apps.apple.com, cgames.com, 163.com, laojia-ip.com; zhihu/NGA/biligame/moegirl were also refused at the proxy). **Every finding below comes from search-engine result summaries of the cited pages, not from full-text reads.** Numbers that several independent outlets repeat are marked higher confidence. 闲鱼 listings themselves could not be accessed or indexed, so all 闲鱼 evidence is from 闲鱼's own reports as covered in the media, or from secondary commentary.
> Evidence-type tags: **[平台自报]** a platform's own report/data; **[媒体转述]** a news outlet relaying data; **[官方]** government or brand-official; **[百科]** Baidu Baike/Wikipedia; **[社区/UGC]** forum, 知乎, 什么值得买 or blogger content; **[推广]** content that reads as promotional; **[推断]** my own inference. ## 1. Platforms and roles: 闲鱼 vs 集换社 vs 卡淘/淘宝/拼多多 vs 抖音直播拆卡 vs 微信/QQ群 vs 线下卡店 (fees, buyer protection, authentication)

### Takeaway
The market splits by function. 集换社 is the TCG-native single-card marketplace and price reference ("集换价", based on its orders). 闲鱼 is the generalist C2C venue: its volume is larger and it skews toward high-ticket graded and collectible cards, but its card price data is unstructured. 卡淘 serves 球星卡 and celebrity cards. 抖音 is the main primary-sale and box-break (拆卡) channel and is under periodic crackdowns. Offline stores handle events and in-person checking. On fees: 闲鱼 charges 0.6% (capped at 60 CNY, with a surcharge for volume sellers; its 鱼小铺 shop tier went to 1.6% uncapped in April 2026), and 集换社 is reported at about 3%. I found no evidence that 闲鱼验货宝 covers trading cards.

### Cited Findings
**闲鱼 (Xianyu) — fees and rules**
- 闲鱼 began charging all sellers a base "软件服务费" of 0.6% per order, capped at 60 CNY per order, from 2024-09-01. Sellers with more than 10 orders in a month and more than 10,000 CNY cumulative pay an extra 1% on the excess. [媒体转述, 中-高] — [36氪](https://www.36kr.com/p/2907214175984518); [腾讯新闻 2024-09-15](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20240915A031LS00); [界面新闻](https://www.jiemian.com/article/11567010.html)
- The fee base includes shipping cost. [社区测算, 中] — [少数派](https://sspai.com/post/92423)
- 2026 change: for "鱼小铺" (shop-tier) sellers, the fee rose from 0.6% to 1.6% of GMV from 2026-04-18. The 60 CNY cap was removed and no pop-up notice was shown. [媒体转述, 中] — [澎湃新闻](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_33090197)
- A 2025 category rule for phones (more than 6 orders a year triggers 1%) shows that 闲鱼 now adds category-specific rules for frequent sellers. I found no card-specific fee rule. [媒体转述, 中] — [网易号](https://163.com/dy/article/JRMBN0I80511AO1U.html)

**闲鱼验货宝 (authentication escrow)**
- 验货宝 is 闲鱼's "verify first, then trade" escrow service. Items go to a verification center built jointly by 闲鱼 and 中国检验认证集团(浙江) (CCIC Zhejiang). [媒体转述, 中] — [36氪](https://www.36kr.com/p/2516606287040769); [IT之家](https://www.ithome.com/0/903/848.htm)
- The categories covered in published guides are 3C/digital, luxury bags and watches, and sneakers/apparel. **No source listed trading cards or graded cards as a 验货宝 category.** [社区/指南, 中] — [新浪 guide](https://k.sina.cn/article_7879923900_1d5ae18bc06801kjnu.html?vt=4&from=tech); [新浪 guide](https://www.sina.cn/news/article/niuypff8382194.html)
- Third-party articles say that if 验货宝 rules an item authentic and it later proves fake, compensation is capped at 3% of the order value, up to 50 CNY. Reported fees vary (19–50 CNY flat, or 1–3% / 1–5% of value), and the sources contradict each other. [社区, 低] — [IT之家](https://www.ithome.com/0/903/848.htm); [新浪](https://sina.cn/detail/nikrvzn9381504)

**集换社 (Jihuanshe) — role and mechanics**
- Operated by 深圳市同调科技有限公司 and launched July 2020. Services: community, selling, auctions, deck building, 寄售 (consignment) and 送评 (submission to graders). Baike claims over 10M registered users by 2023. [百科/官方页面, 中] — [百度百科](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E9%9B%86%E6%8D%A2%E7%A4%BE/67972873); [集换社 关于我们](https://www.jihuanshe.com/about.html)
- The app listing (v3.42.11, updated 2026-10-22) describes free trading of "PTCG、OCG、游戏王、海贼王卡牌等热门TCG", plus 竞拍 (auctions), order-based 行情 prices, and photo or AI card recognition. [平台自报, 高] — [应用宝](https://sj.qq.com/appdetail/com.jihuanshe)
- 集换价 is described as computed from "百万笔历史订单". Community guides advise relying on historical transaction prices, not listing prices or 求购 (buy-order) prices. [社区, 中] — [mwm.ai app summary](https://mwm.ai/apps/tcg/1560760961); [什么值得买 避雷帖](https://post.smzdm.com/p/al3xngvp/)
- Fees: a 2026 知乎 platform comparison quotes a user calling 集换社's "3% 手续费" painful. The same piece reports a DIY (unofficial) card mixed into an order and a refund that took a week of customer-service mediation. **I found no official fee schedule.** [社区, 低] — [知乎 2026 红黑榜](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/1994353542127964679)
- Officially recommended dispute evidence includes a full unboxing video. [社区 relaying official advice, 中] — [什么值得买](https://post.smzdm.com/p/al3xngvp/)

- **懂淘 / 懂游宝 App**: another TCG trading platform that publishes annual data (see Q2). A promotional article pitches it as an alternative to 集换社, claiming "双向资金托管" (two-way escrow) and fees friendlier to bulk sellers. [推广, 低] — [网易](https://c.m.163.com/news/a/L197FNKD0556MFHY.html?spss=expand)

- **抖音直播拆卡**: from January 2024, 抖音 banned box-break rooms from using "玩法" (side games) to attract buyers and required streamers to be authorized by the card brand. A February 2024 campaign targeted 以小博大 / gambling-like break formats. One industry source said the market had saturated by 2024, with "100+ 拆卡 rooms collapsing daily". [媒体转述, 中] — [36氪](https://36kr.com/p/3286483792143232)

- One practitioner quoted in the media puts 拆卡 live-stream margins at "about 30%". [社区/个人说法, 低] — [网易](https://c.m.163.com/news/a/JAIBM2KB051196HN.html)

### Inferences
- [推断] Role map for a reseller. Use 集换社 as the **reference price** and liquidity venue for games it lists. Use 闲鱼 as the **sales venue with more dispersed pricing**: more casual sellers, no structured card data and fewer TCG-savvy buyers. That makes 闲鱼 the likelier place to find mispriced listings, both below 集换价 (buy on 闲鱼, sell on 集换社) and above it (sell to casual buyers on 闲鱼).
- [推断] Fee comparison as of 2026-11. A casual 闲鱼 seller pays 0.6% (≤60 CNY). A volume seller pays 1.6% (鱼小铺, no cap) or 0.6% + 1% on the excess. 集换社 is reported at about 3%. That is roughly a 1.4–2.4 point fee advantage for selling on 闲鱼, offset by slower sales and weaker buyer trust. The 集换社 rate is unverified.
- [推断] Because 验货宝 does not appear to cover cards, buyer protection on 闲鱼 for cards relies on generic escrow (payment held until receipt), graded slabs (third-party certificates) and unboxing videos. That raises counterfeit risk for raw high-value singles on 闲鱼 compared with slabs.

### Gaps
- 集换社's official seller fee, withdrawal fee and consignment fee schedule (the site and app pages were not reachable).
- Whether 闲鱼 has a card-specific category, a card-specific 验货/鉴别 service, or card listing rules as of 2026. Searches found none, but official rules pages could not be read.
- 拼多多 and 微信/QQ群 trading: I found no data on volumes, fees or norms. Group trading is presumably based on reputation and off-platform transfers [推断], but this is unsourced.
- 卡淘's commission and its share of TCG (as opposed to sports and idol cards).

## 2. 集换社 game coverage and rankings, plus 闲鱼 official card data, as popularity proxies

### Takeaway
I could not obtain 集换社's full 2026 game list. The listing and secondary sources confirm Pokémon (PTCG), Yu-Gi-Oh! (OCG), MTG, One Piece CG and Digimon. Riftbound and Lorcana appear in its rankings, and one source adds 王者荣耀卡牌. 集换社's own rankings put **Riftbound** as the strongest new entrant (#3 by GMV in its launch month, Aug 2025; #2 in the composite "2025年下半年中国TCG排行榜"). **One Piece** was the fastest grower in 2024 (+196% orders), followed by Digimon. 闲鱼's December 2025 and September 2026 reports show TCG GMV growth well ahead of the overall card category, increasingly concentrated in ≥5,000 CNY collectible and graded cards.

### Inferences
- [推断] All platform data points the same way. Pokémon dominates value (about 71% of 懂淘 GMV). Yu-Gi-Oh! dominates count but not value. **Below the big three, One Piece, Riftbound and Digimon form the measurable second tier on Chinese platforms.** Lorcana and WS show up only as regional or niche signals.
- [推断] The macro picture is bifurcated: low-price mass collectible cards are shrinking while high-price TCG and graded cards grow on secondary platforms. Monitoring and arbitrage value should therefore sit in mid-to-high-value TCG singles and slabs, not bulk commons.
- [推断] 闲鱼's "domestic graders lead in volume but trail far on price" finding implies a persistent **grader premium gap**: PSA-graded copies of the same card at the same grade sell well above 公博/保粹/卡评 copies.

## 3. Cross-game popularity indicators for ranking second-tier and niche TCGs in mainland China

### Takeaway
Hard platform numbers in China exist only for a handful of games. On the combined evidence, the defensible ordering below the big three is: **Tier 2A: One Piece CG and Riftbound. Tier 2B: Digimon. Tier 3: Lorcana, WS and Gundam CG**, each with partial signals. **Union Arena, Dragon Ball Fusion World, Vanguard, Flesh and Blood, Star Wars Unlimited, WIXOSS and hololive OCG produced no China-specific trading or attention data** in my searches. 卡游-style mass collectible lines (小马宝莉, 叶罗丽, 奥特曼, 哪吒) are huge by volume but form a separate, low-unit-price collectible market.

## 4. Common arbitrage and reselling patterns

### Takeaway
The patterns best supported by evidence are:
1. **Cross-language or cross-region gaps.** Riftbound Chinese boxes cost 240 CNY while English boxes reached about 1,000+ CNY abroad. Japanese Pokémon boxes sell well above 简中 boxes.
2. **Launch scalping followed by restock crashes.** Seen in Riftbound English product and limited gift boxes, and in 简中 Pokémon 30th-anniversary boxes falling through their 360 CNY issue price.
3. **Grading premiums**, which are large for PSA and much weaker for domestic graders.
4. **Single-card buying over box-breaking.** Box EV is usually negative.

Cross-platform gaps (闲鱼 vs 集换社) are plausible but undocumented.

### Cited Findings
**Cross-language / cross-region gaps**
- After the 2025-11-01 English launch of Riftbound, sell-outs drove booster boxes to as much as **$150** on the secondary market, **while the Chinese version retailed at 240 CNY**. [媒体转述, 中-高] — [腾讯新闻 2025-11-03](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20251103A05F8400)
- Later restocks of Origins caused "the deepest price drop since launch". For example, the Proven Grounds gift box fell from $250–300 to a fraction of that once ordering opened. [社区 (旅法师营地), 中] — [旅法师营地](https://www.iyingdi.com/tz/post/5666690); [旅法师营地](https://www.iyingdi.com/tz/post/5678829)
- The limited T1 champion gift box had an official English preorder price of £350–360, yet a US order was reported at **$7,500**. Prices later slid from a peak around £6,000 to about £3,600. A Riftbound Ahri card sold for over $23,200. [社区, 低-中] — [旅法师营地 T1](https://www.iyingdi.com/tz/post/5713915); [旅法师营地 阿狸](https://www.iyingdi.com/tz/post/5713406)
- Pokémon 30th anniversary (released 2026-09-16 worldwide): **简中 box issue price 360 CNY**. Within weeks a domestic secondary listing appeared at **355 CNY with free shipping**, the first break below issue. Japanese boxes were around **¥26,500 JPY**, and US boxes "fell steadily". Japanese retail is lottery-only (抽選), so overseas buyers can only get it at secondary prices. Some 简中 gift-box lottery winners reportedly received no stock while the same box was marked up by scalpers. [社区 (什么值得买), 中] — [什么值得买](https://post.smzdm.com/p/a03x43nr/); [什么值得买](https://post.smzdm.com/p/aggwz26w/); [Buy&Ship](https://www.buyandship.com.tw/blog/pokemon-cards-30th-anniversary-comparison-guide-6435389/)
- Community warning: "同图不等于同款". To avoid false gaps, normalize version, language, currency and transaction date, and compare transaction prices, not listings or buyback quotes. [社区, 中] — [什么值得买](https://post.smzdm.com/p/al38p098/); [什么值得买](https://post.smzdm.com/p/awwopkmm/)
- One Piece example of price dispersion: the same card number can differ about sixfold in price depending on set, grading and character popularity. The top Japanese ask was ¥1,570,000 JPY for OP13-118 Red Comic Parallel Luffy (SNKRDUNK listing, 2026-08-05, ask not sale). [零售商博客, 中] — [Samurai Sword Tokyo](https://samuraiswordtokyo.com/zh-hans/blogs/news/one-piece-card-prices-guide)

**Box-breaking economics (拆盒卖单卡)**
- An HK01 reporter's test: 3 boxes of Japanese M2a plus 1 box of M1 (450 cards) produced no high-value pulls. Each box recovered only about HK$100+, an average loss of over HK$400 per box (≈ over 360 CNY at about 0.9 CNY/HKD [推断 conversion]). A box with one guaranteed SR is normal, and more than one SR counts as "爆配". [媒体实测, 中] — [HK01](https://www.hk01.com/%E7%A4%BE%E6%9C%83%E6%96%B0%E8%81%9E/60330325/pok%C3%A9mon%E5%8D%A1%E8%AE%8A%E9%87%91%E7%A3%9A-%E8%A8%98%E8%80%85%E5%AF%A6%E6%B8%AC-%E7%88%86%E7%9B%92-%E6%8A%BD%E5%8D%A1-%E6%8A%BD%E9%9D%9A%E5%8D%A1%E6%A9%9F%E7%8E%87%E5%83%85%E7%99%BE%E5%88%86%E4%B9%8B%E4%B8%80)
- A box missing its guaranteed SR may have been searched and resealed. Sealed cases are harder to tamper with and guarantee the full ratio of rare cards per box (配率). [媒体, 中] — same HK01 source
- Expected-cost method: single-pack price × 1/(probability of the target card), compared with that card's secondary price. Usually far above market, so buying singles directly is more rational. [社区, 中] — [e书通 数据分析之盲盒](https://www.eshutong.com/archives/48072.html)

**Grading premiums**
- Older (2021) estimates: PSA 10 sells at about 3–5 times the raw card and PSA 9 at about 2 times. A domestic-grader top grade sells at about 1.5–2 times raw and resells less easily. [媒体 2021, 低-中 (dated)] — [虎嗅 2021](https://www.huxiu.com/article/430997.html)
- 知乎 view: domestic graders carry a small premium only on domestic cards and PTCG; for a real premium, use PSA or BGS. Domestic slabs can be opened and the card removed, so the card is not bound to the label. [社区, 低-中] — [知乎](https://www.zhihu.com/question/627571159/answer/3262626247)
- The gap between raw and top-grade cards can reach tens to hundreds of times for scarce cards. A "卡牌修复师" (card restorer) trade has emerged to chase grades. [媒体, 中] — [虎嗅](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4882893.html)
- 2025–2026: PSA raised prices and has backlogs. The FBI broke up a fake-slab ring. [社区, 中] — [什么值得买](https://post.smzdm.com/p/a950ovw5/)

**Reprint, supply and hype shocks**
- The average One Piece 简中 deck price fell from 611 to 329 CNY across 2025 (−46%). [平台自报 via 媒体, 中] — [网易](https://www.163.com/dy/article/L1EG4MSH0556M54F.html)
- Riftbound: a reprint-undersupply investment thesis circulated, then the restock crash followed. [社区, 低-中] — [旅法师营地 重印版供给不足](https://www.iyingdi.com/tz/post/5678083)
- Jennie idol cards (卡淘): about 20,000 trades, then price declines within about 5 days (late Oct 2025). [媒体, 中] — [搜狐](https://sohu.com/a/1096317502_122029351)

### Inferences
- [推断] For China-based reselling, the most structural gap is **简中/中文 product priced in CNY far below overseas English or Japanese secondary prices for the same game at launch** (Riftbound is the clearest case). Exporting is hard for small 闲鱼 sellers, so domestic monitoring captures this mainly as a **leading indicator**: overseas hype pulls up demand for Chinese singles of the same artwork.
- [推断] Launch windows (preorders, lottery shortfalls) give short-lived scalping premiums. Restocks and reprints reliably compress them within weeks, as with Riftbound restocks and Pokémon 30th boxes breaking 360 CNY. A monitor should flag the **issue-price-crossing** event.
- [推断] Cross-platform (闲鱼 vs 集换社) gaps are likeliest where 闲鱼 sellers are casual and price from memory or from Japanese prices, and where 集换社 coverage is thin, as with MTG.

## 5. Which market characteristics suit automated 闲鱼 price monitoring (with concrete examples)

### Takeaway
The best niches combine: (a) standardized card codes, so listings can be matched (e.g. "OP13-118", Gundam set codes such as GD06); (b) an external reference price (集换价, 懂淘, SNKRDUNK or Japanese markets); (c) thin but steady liquidity, with tens to hundreds of trades per month rather than thousands; (d) high price dispersion from language, version, grader and condition; and (e) frequent supply events (new sets, restocks, organized play). On the evidence, **One Piece 简中, Riftbound, Digimon and graded slabs (PSA vs domestic graders)** fit best. **MTG on 闲鱼** is a special case where 集换社 under-covers it. Pokémon is the liquidity benchmark but probably the most crowded.

### Cited Findings
- 集换社's 集换价 is order-based and covers PTCG, OCG, One Piece and other listed games. It provides a reference series for matching 闲鱼 asks. [平台自报, 高] — [应用宝](https://sj.qq.com/appdetail/com.jihuanshe); [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/tcg/1560760961)
- Listing, transaction and buyback prices can coexist and contradict each other, so models must use transaction data. [社区, 中] — [什么值得买](https://post.smzdm.com/p/awwopkmm/)
- Card numbers are standardized, but price depends on set, grade and character popularity (about a 6x spread on the same number for One Piece). [零售商博客, 中] — [Samurai Sword Tokyo](https://samuraiswordtokyo.com/zh-hans/blogs/news/one-piece-card-prices-guide)
- A buyer checklist must capture card name, number, language, version and target condition, plus grader, grade and certificate number for slabs. These map directly onto monitor fields. [社区, 中] — [什么值得买](https://post.smzdm.com/p/al3xngvp/)
- 闲鱼's high-value tilt: cards at 5,000 CNY or more make up over half of GMV, while many low-value cards don't sell. [平台自报 / 社区, 中-高] — [搜狐](https://www.sohu.com/a/1078164702_250147); [什么值得买](https://post.smzdm.com/p/a6zpq2ee/)
- Graded volume on 闲鱼: domestic graders lead on volume but with a large price gap (2025). Graded orders grew +46% in 2025 and about +40% in Jan–Jul 2026. [平台自报, 高] — [网易](https://www.163.com/dy/article/KGE35L1A051795TH.html); [搜狐](https://www.sohu.com/a/1078164702_250147)
- Digimon's seller growth (+23%) outpaces buyer growth (+5%) on 集换社 (2024). [平台自报 via 媒体, 中] — [腾讯新闻](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250222A046PI00)
- Gundam CG has scheduled supply events (GD06 on 2026-10-31) and organized play (Guangzhou, 2026-10-17). [官方微博, 中] — [新浪/官方微博](https://www.sina.cn/news/detail/5348092175715163.html)

### Inferences
- [推断] Concrete monitoring targets, ranked:
  1. **One Piece CG 简中 singles and SPs**: about 230k orders a year on 集换社 (2024 baseline), so liquidity is steady. Standard OPxx-xxx codes. 2025 prices deflated (deck price −46%), so many mispriced listings during repricing. Japanese parallel versions add cross-language dispersion.
  2. **Riftbound Chinese singles**: a new game with the strongest second-tier ranking, Chinese-English price gaps, frequent restock shocks and an active organized-play circuit that moves meta-card prices (e.g. Kai'Sa-deck staples). Highest volatility; best for alerting, not holding.
  3. **Graded slabs across graders**: the same card in PSA vs 公博/保粹/卡评 at the same grade. A large, persistent price gap and rising order growth, though fake slabs are a risk.
  4. **Digimon**: a steady order base and a seller surplus mean asks are dispersed and some sellers dump below the reference price. Good for "below 集换价" sniping.
  5. **MTG on 闲鱼**: trade concentrates outside 集换社, so 闲鱼 asks lack a domestic structured reference. Use international prices (TCGplayer/Cardmarket) as the reference. The Beijing buyer cluster is a possible regional angle.
  6. **Lorcana, WS, Gundam CG**: very thin. Monitor only chase cards and new-set launch windows.
- [推断] Poor fits: bulk commons and low-value cards (illiquid on 闲鱼), 卡游 mass collectible lines (non-standard naming, huge volume, low unit price, child/minor policy risk) and idol/影视 cards (hype half-lives of days, as with Jennie on 卡淘).

### Gaps
- Actual 闲鱼 listing counts or sold counts per game. These are needed to calibrate "thin but steady", and 闲鱼 search is not publicly indexable.
- Whether 闲鱼 permits automated scraping. Terms of service were not checked, and scraping likely violates them [推断]; this needs legal review.

## 6. Risks: counterfeits, 闲鱼/platform scams, platform restrictions, tax/regulatory issues, and 2024–2026 policy on card products for minors

### Takeaway
The main operational risks are counterfeits that now include **fake grading slabs** (2025–2026), resealed or searched boxes, phishing that impersonates platform customer service, and off-platform payment scams. Regulatory exposure comes from three directions:
- **Platform tax reporting.** State Council Decree 810 (2025-06-20) obliges platforms to report seller identity and income quarterly.
- **Minors.** The 2023 盲盒 guideline (no sales to children under 8; guardian consent for ages 8–17) is still the main reference, and is guidance without penalties. 抖音's ongoing child-protection enforcement and the 拆卡 crackdowns since 2024 add pressure.
- **Product safety.** GB 6675-2025 toy-safety rules reportedly cover card accessories for under-14s from 2026-11-01.

I found no 2026 rule aimed specifically at TCG sales to minors.

### Cited Findings
**Counterfeits and fraud**
- Fake PSA slabs come in three forms: fake slab with fake card, real slab with fake card, and cracked-and-resealed real slabs. Fakes copy real certificate numbers and barcodes, so checking the certificate number alone is not enough. Physical checks include edge fogging, looseness, glue traces and a label backlight test. [零售商/媒体 2026, 中] — [Samurai Sword Tokyo](https://samuraiswordtokyo.com/blogs/news/how-to-tell-if-a-psa-slab-is-fake); [HK01 2026](https://www.hk01.com/%E6%95%B8%E7%A2%BC%E7%94%9F%E6%B4%BB/60317001/psa%E9%91%91%E5%AE%9A%E5%8D%A1%E7%9C%9F%E5%81%87%E8%BE%A8%E8%AD%98%E6%94%BB%E7%95%A52026-tcg%E6%94%B6%E8%97%8F%E5%AE%B6%E5%BF%85%E8%AE%80-pokemon-one-piece)
- In January 2026 a US federal jury convicted a defendant of wire fraud for selling sports and Pokémon cards with fake PSA grades. [媒体, 中] — [Samurai Sword Tokyo](https://samuraiswordtokyo.com/blogs/news/how-to-tell-if-a-psa-slab-is-fake)
- Resealed and fake booster packs circulate as prices rise. Tells include heat-melt marks or glue residue at the seal. On singles, check the holo laser pattern (genuine foil flows at about 45° as the card turns) and text sharpness (genuine is 100% K-black; fakes show halftone dots). [媒体, 中] — [HK01](https://www.hk01.com/%E6%95%B8%E7%A2%BC%E7%94%9F%E6%B4%BB/60340931/%E5%AF%B6%E5%8F%AF%E5%A4%A2%E5%8D%A1%E7%89%8C%E7%82%92%E5%83%B9%E7%8B%82%E5%8D%87-%E5%B8%82%E9%9D%A2%E5%85%85%E6%96%A5%E9%87%8D%E5%B0%81%E5%8C%85-%E5%81%87%E5%8D%A1%E5%8C%85-%E5%85%A9%E6%8B%9B%E6%95%99%E4%BD%A0%E5%BF%AB%E9%80%9F%E5%88%86%E8%BE%A8); [楽玩多](https://www.waytofun.com.tw/blogs/news/163259)
- 集换社 phishing: fake "customer service" claims a "商户认证有误" (merchant verification error) and has the victim scan a QR code to a fake page that harvests ID and bank details. Follow-up scams ask for "保证金" (deposits) or "unfreezing" fees. Other pitfall categories: fake or swapped boxes, condition and logistics disputes, and the cost of pursuing claims. [社区, 中] — [什么值得买](https://post.smzdm.com/p/al3xngvp/)
- 闲鱼 example: a buyer lost 8,000 CNY to a "低价清仓" (clearance-price) listing that moved payment off-platform. [社区/推广, 低] — [知乎](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/1994353542127964679)
- Domestic slabs can be opened and the card removed, so the label is not bound to the card. [社区, 低-中] — [知乎](https://www.zhihu.com/question/627571159/answer/3262626247)
